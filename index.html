Why there's no mandatory hosting bill

Most website builders charge you every month whether you need it or not — that's the business model. For the kind of site most small businesses actually need, that monthly fee isn't paying for anything real. Here's exactly what you pay for, what's free, and what isn't included.

What you pay me for

  • Design, development, and copywriting
  • Initial setup and launch
  • Domain connection & configuration
  • Optional future revisions or additions

What's genuinely free

  • Hosting, for qualifying sites, through GitHub Pages
  • No hosting subscription, ever, for that site
  • No paid plugins or platform fees

What's not free — or not included

  • Your domain name (~$12–20/year, paid to a registrar — never free)
  • Online stores, logins, or databases (need a different setup)
  • Ongoing content updates (available, priced separately)

Frequently asked questions

Is hosting really free? +

For sites that qualify — informational and lead-generation sites — yes, hosting is genuinely free through GitHub Pages, for as long as the site exists. The one cost that's never free is your domain name, which you register and own directly, typically $12–20/year.

What if I need an online store or a login system later? +

That's outside what this model is built for. It's not something I'll quietly try to force into a static site — it needs a different setup and a custom conversation, which I'm happy to have if that's where your business is headed.

How long does a project take? +

Roughly 1–2 weeks for a one-page site, 2–3 weeks for a multi-page site, depending on how quickly content comes together on your end.

Do you guarantee more customers or top Google rankings? +

No — and I'd be skeptical of anyone who does. I handle solid, correct on-page SEO basics as part of every project. Rankings and traffic depend on far more than any single website, so I won't promise results I don't control.

What do I need to provide? +

Your business info, a logo if you have one (not required), any existing photos or content, and answers to a short project questionnaire. I'll help organize and write around whatever you send over.

What happens after launch if I need a change? +

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds. After that, additional changes are billed per-request or hourly — never as a recurring subscription just to keep your site running.
